# Recipes and ingredients

## Recipes are shared

Everyone with an account can read every recipe and save a copy under a new name. The copy is yours to change.

The author of a recipe chooses whether others can edit it. Once another Chiro has planned a recipe, it can't change anymore, so their shopping lists stay correct. Save a copy to make your own version.

## Finding and scaling a recipe

The search box on the recipe list filters as you type, on recipe names and ingredients. The ingredient list has the same search.

On a recipe, type a number of portions in *Scale to* and the quantities change as you type. The PDF follows the portions you typed; *Reset* goes back to the recipe's own portions.

## Ingredients are shared too

Every ingredient has one unit: kg, liter or piece. The name and unit are fixed once created, because recipes rely on them. Ask an administrator if one is wrong.

If an ingredient is missing while you're writing a recipe, click *New ingredient* on the recipe form. You won't lose what you typed.
