# Planning a camp

The menu plan is a grid of days and meals. Slots marked in red still need a recipe.

| Meal | Slots |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | main, extra |
| Lunch | soup, main, extra, dessert |
| Tea time | snack |
| Dinner | main, extra, dessert |

Each slot shows the quantities scaled to your camp.

## The draaiboek

The draaiboek collects the practical information, and per day the menu, quantities, recipe steps and your notes. It's available as a page and as a PDF. Fill in the contacts under *Edit camp*.
