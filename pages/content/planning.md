# Planning a camp

The menu plan is a grid of days and meals. Slots marked in red still need a recipe.

| Meal | Slots |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | main, extra |
| Lunch | soup, main, extra, dessert |
| Tea time | snack |
| Dinner | main, extra, dessert |

Each slot shows the quantities scaled to your camp.

## The runbook

The runbook (the cook team's draaiboek) collects the practical information, and per day the menu, quantities, recipe steps and your notes. It's available as a page and as a PDF. Fill in the contacts under *Edit camp*.

## Diets and allergies

Under *Diets and allergies* you enter how many people have which diet. The planner then lists every planned meal that contains something they can't eat. This depends on what ingredients are marked as containing, so always check the labels yourself when it comes to allergies.

## Balance

The planner also flags days without vegetables or fruit, hot meals without vegetables, and days with a lot of sugar. Mark what ingredients contain on the ingredients page, and tick that the list is complete. Until then the planner lists them as not checked.
