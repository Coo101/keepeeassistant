# Getting started

Camp Meal Planner takes you from an empty camp to a day-by-day menu and shopping lists.

1. **Accept your invitation.** The link in your mail adds you to your Chiro. Only members of a Chiro can see its camps.
2. **Create a camp** under *Camps*: dates, how many under-12s, 12+ and staff, and your meal times.
3. **Fill the menu plan.** Click a slot and choose a recipe. Slots only offer recipes that fit.
4. **Choose your shopping days** on the shopping page. You get one list per trip and per shop.
5. **Check the budget**, then upload your bills during or after the camp.

## How portions work

One portion is one 12+ or staff serving. An under-12 counts as 0.7 portion. Recipes and camps both use portions, so quantities scale automatically.

## Your Chiro

Click your name at the top, then your Chiro, to see its members. A Chiro's admins can invite new members and delete camps. Everyone else can plan and change camps. A finished camp can go into the archive, where it stays available.
