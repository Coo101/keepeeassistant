# Bills and what you paid

Upload bills under *Bills* in your camp, as a PDF or a photo.

## Privacy

Bills can hold names, addresses and loyalty card numbers. Crop these out of photos where you can. Every member of your Chiro can open the file. After the review you can remove the file and keep the amounts.

## Reading

PDFs with real text are read right away. Scans and photos wait for an AI model on the administrator's own computer, so this can take a while. You can always add the lines by hand instead.

## The review

Nothing changes until you've checked the lines and saved:

- **Ingredient**: what this line is. Leave it empty for things you don't track.
- **Amount per item**: in the ingredient's unit. A bag of 500 g priced per kg is 0.5.
- **Category**: camp food, activity, personal or other.
- **Skipped**: text that isn't a purchase at all.

A camp-food line with an ingredient sets your camp's price. Your choices are remembered for the next bill from that shop.

## Categories

Only camp food and other (deposits, discounts) count towards the food total you compare with your budget. Pizza for an activity or something personal stays out of it.
